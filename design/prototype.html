安装规则Install rules

1. 看权限Read permissions：列清可访问的域名与目录，不勾不给装domains and paths are listed up front
2. 验签名Verify signature：作者公钥 + 哈希，双击安装也要校验author key + hash, even on double-click
3. 进程隔离Process isolation：sidecar 独立进程，崩溃不带上主网关sidecar crash never takes the gateway down
4. 端口不暴露No exposed port：动态端口只进内部路由表dynamic ports stay internal
